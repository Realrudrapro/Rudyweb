# Code of Conduct

## Our Community

RUDYweb is a community project, and we want it to be a welcoming place for everyone.

By participating in the RUDYweb community, you agree to treat other contributors with respect and help create a positive environment.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate
* Welcome people with different experience levels
* Give constructive feedback
* Accept constructive criticism
* Discuss ideas rather than attacking people
* Respect different opinions
* Help keep discussions productive
* Give credit to other people's work

You do not need to be an experienced developer to contribute to RUDYweb.

Questions are welcome. Mistakes are part of learning.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or bullying
* Personal attacks
* Hate speech or discrimination
* Threats
* Intentionally humiliating or targeting other contributors
* Sexual harassment
* Sharing someone's private information without permission
* Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior
* Malicious contributions or attempts to harm the project

## Disagreements

Disagreements are normal in a community project.

If you disagree with a decision, explain your reasoning respectfully and suggest an alternative when possible.

Maintainers may make the final decision when a discussion cannot reach an agreement.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the RUDYweb project maintainers through the appropriate GitHub reporting or moderation tools.

Please do not publicly share private information about another person when reporting an issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when someone violates this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

* A warning
* Removing inappropriate content
* Closing discussions or issues
* Restricting participation
* Removing someone from the project

Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from participating in the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to RUDYweb's GitHub repository, Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other official community spaces associated with the project.

## Final Note

RUDYweb is built by a community.

Be respectful. Help others. Build cool things. 🚀

**RUDYweb — bringing the web closer.**
